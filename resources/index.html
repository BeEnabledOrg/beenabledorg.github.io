Resource Hub & Calendar

Disability programs, services, and events from across Ohio, in one place you can search.

In short

This page lists disability programs, services, and events from Ohio counties and agencies.

You can search by county, topic, and disability type.

Our team checked and added most of what is here so far from each organization's own website; agencies can also tell us about a program using our form.

This page is new and still growing, so most Ohio counties do not have a listing yet.

What this is

Finding disability services in Ohio usually means checking one county at a time, or one agency at a time. The Resource Hub brings that information into one searchable place instead.

It covers ongoing programs, such as a weekly peer support group, and one-time events, such as a benefits workshop. Each one is tagged with its county, its topic, and the kind of disability it serves, so you can search by whichever of those matters to you.

We are building this out gradually. Every one of Ohio's 88 counties is already in the search below, even the ones without a listing yet.

How a program gets listed here

Agencies and counties do not post directly to this page.

  1. An agency tells us about their program using the submission form — or our own team finds and checks one directly on the organization's own site, which is how most listings here got added so far.
  2. Our team checks it for accuracy and plain language.
  3. Once we approve it, it appears here for everyone to find. If we need a change first, we send an agency submission back with a note.

Every listing links back to the organization's own site, so you can always confirm details there before you go. Know a program that should be here? Submit a program, whether or not you run it yourself.
