Tell us your medical 911 story

You called 911, or someone called for you. What happened next?

In short

Tell us about one time 911 was called for a medical emergency.

It can be good, bad, or mixed. Every story counts.

You do not need to give your name.

It takes about 10 minutes. Only the story is required.

This is not a way to get help in an emergency.

If you need help right now, call 911. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, call or text 988.

Why this topic first

When disabled people call 911, things can go wrong in ways other people never face.

  • A wheelchair or other equipment gets left behind.
  • Responders cannot understand how someone communicates.
  • Police come instead of, or along with, medical help.
  • A service animal is kept away.
  • Medicine or a device does not come to the hospital.

We hear these stories all the time. But almost nobody counts them. We want to.

This connects to our Blue Envelope Project, which helps drivers share their needs during a traffic stop.

Your story

Every box tells you what it is for. Only the first one is required. Skip anything you do not want to answer.

Please leave out names of people, hospitals, and streets. We remove them anyway, but it is safer not to send them.

Start wherever you like. What was going on, who came, how they treated you, and how it ended. There is no wrong way to write it.

A few short questions

These are what let us count stories and spot patterns. Each one is optional.

Who called 911? (optional)

Pick the one closest to what happened.

When did this happen? (optional)

A rough guess is fine.

So we can compare places.

Leave this blank if it is a small place where people might know who you are.

Who came? (optional, choose all that apply)

Everyone who showed up, as far as you know.

Did they understand your disability and what you needed? (optional)

Think about how they spoke to you, moved you, and listened.

Did any of these happen? (optional, choose all that apply)

These are things we hear about often. Choose any that fit.

If you could change one thing about how it went, what would it be?

How we may use your story

Can we share some of your words? (optional)

We always count your answers. This is only about quoting what you wrote, with nothing that names you.

Only if you would like us to follow up, or to hear what we learn. We keep it apart from your story.

Your privacy

  • You do not need to give your name.
  • A person on our team removes anything that could point to you before your story is counted or quoted.
  • If you give an email address, we keep it apart from your story.
  • We never sell what you tell us.
  • If you gave an email address, you can ask us to delete your story at any time.
